# Panel Voices Settings

A list of your account's voices, each with its own toggle. Toggled voices are the only ones shown in the **panel's** Voice dropdown.

- **Leave none selected** to show every voice in the panel.
- If voices have not loaded yet, the group says so. Set an API key and use the refresh button in [[ElevenLabs Settings]].

> [!tip] Why bother
> ElevenLabs accounts can have dozens of voices. A shortlist of three or four keeps the panel quick to use.
